## The Three Dimensions of Every Color

| Dimension | What It Governs | The Question It Answers |
|---|---|---|
| **Transparency** | The open field — all the color contains, offers, radiates freely | *What does this color give freely?* |
| **Clarity** | Depth made navigable — the color at its most precisely legible | *What does this color make clear?* |
| **Opacity** | The protected interior — what the color withholds, guards, requires initiation to enter | *What does this color protect?* |

The tablet is the **law** beneath all three dimensions. The three color documents are the **expressions** of that law across the three phases of encounter.
