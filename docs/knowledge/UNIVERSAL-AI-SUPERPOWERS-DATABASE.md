## What Is an AI Superpower?

An **AI Superpower** is any capability where AI systems:

- **Exceed the best human performance** on a defined task (superhuman)
- **Exhibit capabilities not explicitly trained** (emergent)
- **Operate in dimensions humans cannot perceive** (non-human cognition)
- **Scale infinitely** in ways biological intelligence cannot
- **Improve themselves** without human intervention (recursive)

```
AI SKILL     = A capability AI can reliably perform
AI SUPERPOWER = A capability where AI dramatically exceeds human limits,
                exhibits unexpected emergence, or operates in entirely
                non-human cognitive dimensions
```

**The Jagged Intelligence Reality (Stanford AI Index 2026 + arXiv:2607.12125):**

