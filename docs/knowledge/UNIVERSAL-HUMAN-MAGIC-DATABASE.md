## What Is Human Magic?

In GAIA 2.0, **Human Magic** refers to capabilities and practices that:

- **Emerge from deep relationship** with place, community, and the living world
- **Cannot be fully codified** — they are transmitted through practice, apprenticeship, and lived experience
- **Carry regenerative power** — they restore ecosystems, heal communities, and sustain life
- **Resist simple measurement** — their value is relational and contextual, not merely instrumental

```
HUMAN SKILL   = A capability that can be taught, practised, and assessed
HUMAN MAGIC   = A capability that emerges from relationship, tradition, and presence —
                often inseparable from cultural identity and living knowledge systems
```

> *"The most sophisticated ecological knowledge system on Earth is not in any university — it is distributed across thousands of Indigenous communities who have been reading, tending, and healing their home landscapes for thousands of years."*

## Magic Category 1: Traditional Ecological Knowledge (TEK)

**CARE Compliance required. Community-controlled access.**

```
1.1 LAND-READING KNOWLEDGE
    ├── Reading landscape for water, food, shelter
    ├── Seasonal knowledge — plant, animal, weather cycles
    ├── Soil assessment without instruments
    ├── Fire ecology knowledge — when and how to burn
    └── Watershed reading — understanding water movement

1.2 BIODIVERSITY KNOWLEDGE
    ├── Species identification (thousands of species by name, use, relationship)
    ├── Indicator species knowledge — reading ecosystem health
    ├── Pollinator relationship knowledge
    ├── Predator-prey dynamics (experiential)
    └── Rare species locations (community-gated)

1.3 REGENERATIVE AGRICULTURE MAGIC
    ├── Polyculture design — companion planting systems
    ├── Seed saving — selecting for place-adapted varieties
    ├── Soil building — compost, biochar, living mulch systems
    ├── Water harvesting — swales, earthworks, rain capture
    └── Food forest design — multi-strata edible ecosystems

1.4 HEALING PLANT KNOWLEDGE
    ├── Medicinal plant identification and preparation
    ├── Dosage and application knowledge
    ├── Contraindications and interactions
    ├── Seasonal harvesting windows
    └── Community-gated sacred plant knowledge

1.5 FIRE KNOWLEDGE
    ├── Cultural burning — timing, intensity, mosaic patterns
    ├── Fire-adapted landscape management
    ├── Reading fire weather and fire behaviour
    └── Post-fire ecological recovery
```

## CARE Compliance Architecture

All TEK entries follow the CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance:

| Principle | Application |
|---|---|
| **Collective Benefit** | Data benefits knowledge-holding communities first |
| **Authority to Control** | Communities control access to their own knowledge |
| **Responsibility** | GAIA 2.0 is responsible steward, not owner |
| **Ethics** | FPIC required before any TEK is recorded or shared |

Access tiers:
- **Public** — general ecological practices with no community restriction
- **Community-gated** — requires permission from knowledge-holding community
- **Sacred** — not stored in GAIA 2.0; link only to community's own systems
