# Free developer plane

Power to people: anyone can build on the local SOS plane. Revenue does not gate compile or run.

Listed numbers (not a payment processor):

- Upgrade offer after **5000** declared units of revenue.
- Upgrade price **1000** units. Optional. Refusal does not revoke build.
- Commission **500 bps (5%)** of declared revenue on both free and upgraded paths. Listed, not collected in this crate.
- Paid amplification of governance voice stays **false**.

Refuse: turning the plane into a store that locks out free users, weapons targeting, child accounts as marketplace inventory.
