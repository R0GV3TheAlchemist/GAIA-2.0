# GAIA geometry modules

**Status:** first cut  
**Crate:** `gaia-geometry`  
**Does not close** #24, #25, #311, or tag v1.0.0

## What is true

Formal geometry (circle, vesica intersection, diamond gates, compass questions) plus a First-Law registry. A symbol without a complete contract is not operational.

## What is not true

- Sacred geometry as physics or causal power
- A circle that commands people or nature
- An aggregate flourishing / morality score
- TEK or living-tradition extraction
- Sentience, ASI, or a geometry v1.0 product

See also `gaia-spec/governance/golden-compass/` (#311) and the Magic Circle of Knowledge (#312).
