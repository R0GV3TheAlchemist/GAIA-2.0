# Viriditas and the Unfinished Horizon

## Viriditas — tradition-contextual term

*Viriditas* is a Latin term associated especially with Hildegard of Bingen, commonly glossed as greenness or greening power within a theological and cosmological vocabulary of vitality. In GAIA it is **not** a universal scientific quantity, biological law, moral score, or proof of supernatural causation.

This specification uses it only as a tradition-contextual name, then translates the operational test as generative knowledge:

> Does this knowledge increase understanding, agency, capability, resilience, repair, and future possibility without concealing harm?

## Operational translation

| Generative question | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Understanding | What can be understood more clearly, including limits? |
| Agency | Whose capacity to act, refuse, or revise is increased or reduced? |
| Capability | What skill or means becomes possible, and for whom? |
| Resilience | What recovery or robustness is supported? |
| Repair | What harm can be named and addressed? |
| Future possibility | What options remain open for people and living systems? |
| Concealed harm | What injury, extraction, or erasure would this record hide? |

A record that conceals harm, flattens a living tradition, or presents stolen material as open knowledge FAILS the generative test regardless of claimed benefit.

## Unfinished Horizon

There is always more to learn. Every conforming record MUST include:

1. Uncertainty — what is not known or not warranted.
2. Knowledge gaps — what has not been looked for, or cannot be looked for here.
3. Dissent — disagreement that remains on the record, or an explicit statement that none is recorded yet.
4. Revision capability — how the record can be qualified, contested, corrected, retracted, or withdrawn.

The Horizon forbids closure language such as “complete knowledge of X” or “GAIA has learned everything needed.” Curiosity remains a duty, not a claim of unfinishedness as property.

## Prohibition

Do not treat Viriditas as a Church doctrine imposed on other traditions, as a wellness product, as a ranking of people, or as a substitute for evidence. Do not close the Horizon to protect a preferred model.
