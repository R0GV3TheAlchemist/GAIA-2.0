# Universal Symbology Bridge

## Purpose

The Bridge connects meaning-bearing signs to the Magic Circle without collapsing categories. It extends `gaia-spec/governance/golden-compass/symbolic-traditions-boundary.md`. A symbol may be studied, contextualized, or user-declared. A symbol does not create authority, prove empirical causation, determine identity, or authorize action.

## Rule

Operational use requires the First Law of Symbolic Accountability: defined process, inputs, outputs, accountable steward, constraints, evidence/metric basis, and feedback/correction path. If that mapping is absent, `operational_claim` MUST be false.

## Category boundaries

| Category | Permitted GAIA role | Prohibited role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formal geometry | Computation, visualization, education | Metaphysical or empirical overclaim |
| Historical alchemy | History of science, material-symbol research, art | Modern chemistry substitute or causal claim |
| Astronomy / zodiac history | Astronomy education, calendar/history interface | Personality inference or factual prediction |
| Religious / spiritual traditions | Contextual scholarship and user-declared meaning | Extraction, flattening, automatic use, authority claim |
| Indigenous / community-held knowledge | Only with applicable authority and collective consent | Scraping, publication, training, or inference without grant |
| Fiction | Narrative study and metaphor-only interface | Labeling people or deciding real-world moral or empirical worth |
| Personal / user-declared symbolism | Local/private expression by default | Inference of identity, belief, health, or authority |
| Empirical claim | Evidence-bearing knowledge record in a scientific or observational method | Mixing unmapped symbols into the evidence field |

Category collapse is a specification defect. Formal mathematics, historical symbolism, living traditions, fiction, personal meaning, and empirical claims MUST remain separately labeled even when they share a visual motif.

## Magic-circle boundary

A GAIA circle MAY visually represent context, relationship, permitted scope, and protected boundaries. It MUST NOT be presented as a means to command people, entities, nature, or outcomes. A circle never substitutes for consent, legitimate authority, evidence, or safety controls.

## Record requirements

When a record uses a symbol:

1. Name the category.
2. State whether an operational claim is made.
3. If operational, include the First Law mapping.
4. If not operational, state the permitted role (art, history, education, personal meaning).
5. Refuse personality inference, astrology-based prediction, and supernatural-causation claims.
