# Diamond of Knowledge

```text
                       Truth
                         ▲
                         │
      Memory ◀────── ◇ ──────▶ Discovery
                         │
                         ▼
                   Understanding
```

Wisdom integrates the four points under context, uncertainty, dissent, and legitimate authority. Wisdom is not a fifth score and not a substitute for consent.

## Purpose

The Diamond is a structured conversation for knowledge records, not an automated truth or ranking score. It is distinct from the Golden Compass Diamond of Alignment (Truth, Care, Growth, Balance). Alignment asks whether to act. Knowledge asks what is warranted to hold, remember, seek, and understand.

| Dimension | Required question |
| --- | --- |
| Truth | What is claimed, on what evidence, and what would count against it? |
| Memory | What sources, attributions, prior records, and withdrawals must be retained? |
| Discovery | What remains unknown, untested, unheard, or outside current methods? |
| Understanding | What model or interpretation is offered, and where does it fail? |
| Wisdom | What bounded, humble, and revisable stance is appropriate under uncertainty? |

## Gate results

Each dimension returns one of: `hold`, `qualify`, `contest`, `defer`, or `withdraw`.

- `hold`: the record may be kept at its stated epistemic status with sources intact.
- `qualify`: the record may be kept only with narrower scope, weaker status, or added uncertainty.
- `contest`: dissent or counter-evidence MUST remain attached.
- `defer`: evidence, consent, or community authority is insufficient for the proposed status or publication.
- `withdraw`: the record MUST be retracted, sealed, or not stored.

A record MUST `withdraw` or `defer` when restricted knowledge lacks applicable authority, when sources cannot be distinguished from interpretation, or when a symbol is presented as operational without First Law mapping.

## Prohibition

Implementations MUST NOT collapse the Diamond into one knowledge, intelligence, or wisdom score. They MAY present structured evidence, uncertainty, dissent, and review requirements.
