# Round Table of Knowledge

## Principle

Knowledge is held in relation. No participant owns the epistemic center. The Round Table is a governance metaphor for shared standing, attribution, challenge, and correction. It is not an assertion of historical perfection and not a license for extraction.

## Required practices

| Practice | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Attribution | Sources, authors, communities, translators, and prior records MUST be named or explicitly marked unknown. |
| Dialogue | Competing interpretations MAY coexist on one record without forced merger. |
| Debate | Challenge MUST be representable without deleting the challenged claim. |
| Peer review | Method-appropriate review SHOULD be recorded when a knowledge claim is offered beyond personal notes. |
| Correction | Errors MUST have a path to qualify, contest, or replace a record without silent rewrite of provenance. |
| Retraction | A claim MAY be marked retracted; retracted text remains auditable unless `do-not-store` applies. |
| Withdrawal | Applicable community or rights-holders MAY require withdrawal, sealing, or destruction. |
| Community authority | Living traditions and community-held knowledge are governed by those communities, not by GAIA default publication rules. |

## Authority rules

1. Epistemic status does not create authority to publish, train, or act.
2. Community authority, consent, and withdrawal beat convenience of retrieval.
3. Dissent MUST remain visible on contested public records.
4. Correction MUST preserve prior provenance. Learning MUST NOT silently rewrite sources, authorization, or personal data.
5. AI systems may retrieve, compare, warn, and propose. They MUST NOT claim final knowledge authority or close the Unfinished Horizon.

## Outcomes

- `record`: keep at stated status with attribution intact.
- `qualify`: narrow status, scope, or publication.
- `contest`: attach dissent and keep both sides auditable.
- `retract`: mark the claim withdrawn from active use.
- `seal`: retain only under restricted/community-governed rules.
- `destroy`: honor `do-not-store` or a valid withdrawal that forbids retention.

## Prohibition

The Round Table MUST NOT be used to override valid refusal, TEK seals, child safety, law, or community sovereignty. It MUST NOT harvest sacred or restricted knowledge for completeness.
