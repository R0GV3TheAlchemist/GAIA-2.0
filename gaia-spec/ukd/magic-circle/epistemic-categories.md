# Epistemic Categories

## Rule

Every knowledge record MUST distinguish epistemic status and sources from interpretation and application. A later status does not erase earlier ones. Promotion along the chain MUST be explicit, sourced, and reversible through correction.

## Categories

| Status | Meaning | Required distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observation / data | Recorded signal, measurement, utterance, or artifact as encountered | Provenance, scope, time, instrument/method if any, and known gaps |
| Information | Data placed in a stated context or relation | Context is interpretation-adjacent and MUST remain labeled |
| Evidence | Information offered to support or challenge a claim | What it is evidence *for*, quality limits, and counter-evidence |
| Knowledge claim | A statement held as warranted for a stated community or method | Warrant, dissent, and the community or method that accepts it |
| Interpretation | Meaning assigned to sources or claims | MUST be stored apart from sources; MUST NOT overwrite them |
| Understanding / model | A structured account of relations, causes, or meanings | Assumptions, scope of applicability, and failure conditions |
| Wisdom / judgment | Bounded judgment under uncertainty, values, and authority | Who judges, with what standing, and what remains unknown |
| Stewardship / application | Use of judgment to care for people, places, knowledge, or systems | Consent, authority, harm, repair path, and review |

## Transformation chain

```text
Data → Information → Evidence → Knowledge → Understanding → Wisdom → Stewardship
```

1. The chain is not a one-way ladder. Understanding MAY return to Evidence. Stewardship MAY generate new Observation.
2. Evidence MUST NOT be collapsed into Information or Knowledge.
3. Wisdom and Stewardship MUST NOT be inferred from volume of data.
4. Absence of a later status is valid. A record MAY remain Observation, Evidence, or a contested Knowledge claim.

## Learning paths

| Path | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery | What is newly noticed, sought, or still missing? |
| Memory | What is retained, attributed, sealed, forgotten, or withdrawn? |
| Understanding | What model or meaning is offered, and under what uncertainty? |
| Application | What, if anything, may be done, by whom, and with what repair path? |

## Library Ring

A record SHOULD name one or more Library Ring domains: History, Science, Art, Nature, Ethics, Philosophy, Engineering, Human Experience, Place, Language/oral tradition. Domain membership is classification for retrieval and plurality. It is not a rank of worth and MUST NOT flatten living traditions into a single science-or-fiction binary.

## Prohibition

Implementations MUST NOT auto-ingest restricted knowledge, auto-promote epistemic status, or present a pipeline output as truth.
