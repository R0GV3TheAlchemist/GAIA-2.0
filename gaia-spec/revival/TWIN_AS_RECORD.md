# Twin as record (#373)

<!-- gaia-claim class=symbolic id=twin-as-record-label -->

A twin in this repo is a **governed record plus consent**. It is a file the human can export, revoke, and delete. It is not a moral patient, not a legal person, and not a consciousness.

<!-- gaia-claim class=prohibited id=twin-as-person -->

Calling something a GAIAN or Earth Twin does not mint rights, feelings, or agency. Peer envelopes still cannot carry vault or raw memory (`VaultDumpDenied`). Photos still cannot auto-fill identity (`PhotoInference`).
