# The Secret Doctrine — extract, not a source to invoke

H. P. Blavatsky, 1888. Two volumes in her lifetime: Cosmogenesis and Anthropogenesis. Framed as commentary on verses she attributed to a hidden *Book of Dzyan* in a language she called Senzar. No manuscript of that book has been produced. Critics mapped stretches to 19th-century print (*Vishnu Purana*, geology, Atlantis literature). Reception later includes occult fiction and UFO writing. None of that is a GAIA dependency.

Do not paste the book here. Summarize and bind.

## Three propositions (the hangers)

1. **Immutable Principle** — omnipresent, boundless, beyond thought (*Parabrahman* / Be-ness). Speculation dwarfs it. We do not name this as an actuator or a fifth product.
2. **Periodicity** — the universe in toto as a boundless plane; worlds appear and vanish (sparks of Eternity). Cycle, not a ship date.
3. **Identity of souls with the Over-Soul** plus an obligatory pilgrimage through forms under karma. Spark walks rungs and grows individuality. That is a *cycle in a book*, not a job title and not twin-as-person.
