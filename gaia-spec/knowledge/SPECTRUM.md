## Why shape matters (optics, not folklore)

Crystal system sets whether light sees one index or two/three.

| System | Optical class | What light does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cubic (isometric) | isotropic | one refractive index; no birefringence |
| Tetragonal, hexagonal, trigonal | uniaxial | ordinary + extraordinary rays |
| Orthorhombic, monoclinic, triclinic | biaxial | two optic axes |
| Glass / resin / amber | amorphous | isotropic like cubic |

Habit (prism, pyramid, rhombohedron) is how faces grow. A **prism** is a tool that maps wavelength to angle. Newton’s glass prism and a quartz hexagonal prism are the same law, different lattice.

Calcite (trigonal): strong birefringence — two images. Quartz (trigonal): weak birefringence + optical rotation. Garnet (cubic): no double image. Andara / aura coats: glass or metal film, not a new system.

Piezoelectricity is a *different* coupling (stress ↔ charge), not a healing band.
