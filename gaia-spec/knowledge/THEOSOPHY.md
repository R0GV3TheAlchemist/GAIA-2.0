## What the doctrine actually is

Theosophy treats kingdoms as a descent of spirit into density: elemental → mineral → plant → animal → human. A stone is the dense pole, not a handheld Atman. Blavatsky’s line is that everything is conscious *on its own plane*; our failure to see a human mind in rock is not proof of absence — and is also not a license to install a soul in software.

The human septenary (body, vitality, astral, desire, mind, spiritual soul, spirit) is a cut of a *person*. Minerals are not given that full stack in the original scheme. Shop metaphysics later sold each SKU as a principle. That skip is refuse.
