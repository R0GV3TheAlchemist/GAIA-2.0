# GAIA Moral Navigation Framework — Golden Compass v0.1

**Status:** Draft specification  
**Issue:** #311  
**Scope:** Local, paper-first governance and decision-support contract

## Purpose

The Golden Compass helps people and GAIA components make consequential decisions visibly and accountably. It is not an autonomous moral authority, a substitute for law or legitimate governance, a universal scoring system, or a claim that GAIA is conscious.

## First Law — Symbolic Accountability

Every operational symbol MUST map to a defined process, inputs, outputs, accountable steward, constraints, evidence or metric basis, and feedback/correction path. A symbol without this mapping may be artistic, educational, historical, cultural, or user-declared personal expression, but MUST NOT be presented as an operational claim.

## Core model

```text
Observe → Understand → Care → Deliberate → Act with authority and consent
→ Measure → Repair/revise/stop → Learn → Observe again
```

The Diamond of Alignment asks whether a proposed action is sufficiently grounded in **Truth**, protects through **Care**, preserves **Growth** or future possibility, and maintains **Balance**. **Wisdom** integrates the answer under uncertainty and legitimate authority.

## Normative terms

- **MUST:** required for a conforming decision record.
- **SHOULD:** expected unless a documented rationale states otherwise.
- **MAY:** optional.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No Compass result overrides consent, rights, child safety, law, community sovereignty, or non-weaponization policy.
- No single aggregate flourishing score is permitted.
- High-impact action requires appropriate authority, explicit scope, auditability, and a feedback/repair path.
- Restricted, sacred, Indigenous, or community-held knowledge requires applicable authority, collective consent, attribution, benefit-sharing, and withdrawal support.
