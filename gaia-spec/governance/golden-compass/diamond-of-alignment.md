# Diamond of Alignment

```text
                       Truth
                         ▲
                         │
      Balance ◄──────── ◇ ────────► Growth
                         │
                         ▼
                        Care
```

## Purpose

The Diamond is a structured conversation, not an automated morality score. It makes four necessary decision dimensions explicit and asks Wisdom to integrate them under uncertainty, consent, legitimate authority, and feedback.

| Dimension | Required question |
| --- | --- |
| Truth | What evidence supports the claim, and what remains uncertain? |
| Care | Who or what may be harmed; what rights, consent, dignity, and protection are required? |
| Growth | What future capability, resilience, learning, opportunity, or life may become possible? |
| Balance | What tradeoffs, limits, dependencies, inequities, and stability conditions apply? |
| Wisdom | What bounded, legitimate, proportional, reversible, and accountable path is appropriate? |

## Gate results

Each dimension returns one of: `pass`, `narrow`, `defer`, `escalate`, or `refuse`.

A decision MUST be `refuse` when a hard constraint is violated. A decision MUST be `defer` or `escalate` when evidence, consent, authority, or repair planning is insufficient for the proposed scope.

## Prohibition

Implementations MUST NOT collapse the Diamond into one numerical moral or flourishing score. They MAY present structured evidence, uncertainty, tradeoffs, and human/community review requirements.
