# Symbolic Traditions Boundary

## Purpose

GAIA may support respectful study, contextual description, art, and user-declared personal exploration of symbolic systems. It MUST distinguish formal mathematics, empirical science, historical alchemy, astronomy, astrology, living religious traditions, Indigenous/community-held knowledge, contemporary esotericism, fiction, and personal symbolism.

## Rule

A symbol does not create authority, prove empirical causation, determine a person's identity, or authorize action. Operational use requires the First Law of Symbolic Accountability: a defined process, inputs, outputs, accountable steward, constraints, evidence/metric basis, and feedback/correction path.

## Categories

| Category | Examples | Permitted GAIA role | Prohibited role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Formal geometry | circles, lattices, torus, polyhedra | computation, visualization, education | metaphysical or empirical overclaim |
| Historical alchemy | elemental and planetary-metal notation | history of science, material-symbol research, art | modern chemistry substitute or causal claim |
| Astronomy and zodiac history | ecliptic, constellations, historical zodiac symbols | astronomy education, calendar/history interface | personality inference or factual prediction |
| Religious and spiritual traditions | yantras, mandalas, sacred diagrams | contextual scholarship and user-declared meaning | extraction, flattening, automatic use, authority claim |
| Indigenous/community-held knowledge | place-based and ceremonial knowledge | only with applicable authority and collective consent | scraping, publication, training, or inference without grant |
| Fictional systems | games, fantasy schools, alignment taxonomies | narrative study and metaphor-only interface | labeling people or deciding real-world moral worth |
| Personal symbolism | a user's declared meaning | local/private expression by default | inference of identity, belief, health, or authority |

## Alchemical Elements Circle

The optional Alchemical Elements Circle is a visual map of transformation, not a supernatural mechanism. Historical symbols may be mapped to real system questions:

- Fire: energy-dependent transformation; declare energy source, inputs, outputs, safety, and emissions/waste.
- Water: flow and circulation; declare use, quality, restoration, and distribution impacts.
- Air: communication and exchange; declare information flow, access, sensing, and atmospheric effects when applicable.
- Earth: materiality and stability; declare materials, place, lifecycle, repairability, and end-of-life path.

The real dependency layer remains Energy, Water, Atmosphere, Matter, Life, and Time.

## Magic-circle boundary

A GAIA circle MAY visually represent context, relationship, permitted scope, and protected boundaries. It MUST NOT be presented as a means to command people, entities, nature, or outcomes. A circle never substitutes for consent, legitimate authority, evidence, or safety controls.

## Publication boundary

Every Compass artifact MUST declare one status:

- `private-draft`
- `approved-for-public-spec`
- `approved-for-public-example`
- `restricted`
- `community-governed`
- `do-not-store`

Only artifacts explicitly approved for public use MAY be committed to a public repository. Public artifacts MUST exclude private personal material and respect applicable cultural/community authority, attribution, benefit-sharing, and withdrawal requirements.
