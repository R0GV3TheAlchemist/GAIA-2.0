# Polarity Register

## Purpose

The Polarity Register records dynamic tensions. It does not treat either pole as intrinsically good or require a fixed midpoint.

| Tension | Failure at pole A | Failure at pole B | Navigation objective |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Order ↔ variation | Rigidity, monoculture, inability to adapt | Fragmentation, unsafe unpredictability | Adaptive order |
| Growth/exploration ↔ preservation | Overshoot, extraction, irreversible harm | Stagnation, missed adaptation | Sustainable evolution |
| Logic ↔ compassion | Cold optimization, rights-blind efficiency | Unintended consequences, ineffective care | Wise care |
| Individual agency ↔ collective wellbeing | Isolation, externalized harm | Coercion, erased consent | Interdependent flourishing |
| Analysis ↔ imagination | Reductionism, lost possibility | Unsupported claims, impracticality | Grounded creativity |
| Freedom ↔ responsibility | Harm shifted to others | Paternalism, loss of agency | Accountable freedom |

## Record requirements

A Compass decision SHOULD name active tensions, describe their context, identify who bears each cost or benefit, list alternatives, and explain why the chosen scope is proportionate. A polarity record MUST NOT be used to override hard constraints, consent, or legitimate authority.
