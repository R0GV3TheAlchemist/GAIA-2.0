1. Binding UN AI body and cross-border inspectors.
2. Corporate ISO 42001 compliance product.
3. Named third-party runtimes (TELOS, nine-layer stacks) as if they were ours.

5. Live disaster-response operations or fuel-phaseout programs.

Those belong to states, standards bodies, and operators. GAIA can *list* them. It cannot *be* them.
