GAIA 2.0 is built on a constitutional commitment to indigenous sovereignty (Invariant 0.7). This is not a policy. It is an unbreakable requirement. But what does indigenous sovereignty mean in practice — in code, in data systems, in AI models, in GAIAN conversations?

The **CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance** — Collective Benefit, Authority to Control, Responsibility, and Ethics — are the answer. Developed by the **Global Indigenous Data Alliance (GIDA)** in 2019 and now adopted by governments, universities, and research institutions worldwide, the CARE Principles provide the most comprehensive framework for indigenous data governance ever created.

## PART I: THE CARE PRINCIPLES — WHAT THEY ARE

### 1.1 Origin and Definition

The CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance were drafted at the **International Data Week and Research Data Alliance Plenary** co-hosted event "Indigenous Data Sovereignty Principles for the Governance of Indigenous Data Workshop," **November 8, 2018, Gaborone, Botswana**.

They were published in the **Data Science Journal** in 2020 and have since been translated into Spanish, Vietnamese, Māori, German, and Khmer.

**The Four CARE Principles:**

```
C — COLLECTIVE BENEFIT
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"Data ecosystems shall be designed and function in ways that enable
Indigenous Peoples to derive benefit from the data."

Three sub-principles:
C1: For inclusive development and innovation
    Indigenous data are vital to Indigenous innovation and self-determination.
    Data ecosystems must be designed to enable Indigenous Peoples to create
    value from their data in ways grounded in Indigenous worldviews.

C2: For improved governance and citizen engagement
    Data enrich the planning, implementation, and evaluation of policies
    and programs that serve Indigenous communities. Data must be made
    available and accessible to Indigenous institutions and governments
    to support Indigenous governance.

C3: For equitable outcomes
    Indigenous data are not used against Indigenous Peoples. Harms and
    asymmetries must be addressed. Data must be used to advance equity
    and justice for Indigenous Peoples.

GAIA 2.0 Implementation:
- Indigenous communities benefit from GAIA 2.0 Earth Twin data
- GAIAN serves indigenous communities in their languages
- Earth stewardship data benefits indigenous land managers
- No indigenous data used to harm indigenous communities

A — AUTHORITY TO CONTROL
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"Indigenous Peoples' rights and interests in Indigenous data must be
recognised, and their authority to control such data must be empowered."

Three sub-principles:
A1: Recognising rights and interests
    Indigenous Peoples have rights to their data, including the right
    to create, collect, access, analyze, interpret, manage, share,
    and govern their data.

A2: Data for governance
    Indigenous Peoples have the right to data that are relevant to
    their worldviews and empower self-determination and effective
    self-governance. Indigenous data must be made available to
    Indigenous nations and communities in order to support Indigenous
    governance.

A3: Governing Indigenous data
    Indigenous Peoples have the right to develop cultural governance
    protocols for Indigenous data and be active leaders in the
    stewardship of, and access to, Indigenous data, especially in
    the context of Indigenous Knowledge.

GAIA 2.0 Implementation:
- Indigenous communities have VETO POWER over their data in GAIA 2.0
- Sacred knowledge is NEVER shared without community consent
- Indigenous communities control their own GAIAN data
- Indigenous Council has binding authority in GAIA 2.0 governance

R — RESPONSIBILITY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"Those working with Indigenous data have a responsibility to share
how those data are used to support Indigenous Peoples' self-determination
and collective benefit."

Three sub-principles:
R1: For positive relationships
    Indigenous data use is unviable unless it is coupled with
    relationships built on respect, reciprocity, trust, and mutual
    understanding, as defined by the Indigenous Peoples to whom
    those data relate.

R2: For expanding capability and capacity
    Use of Indigenous data invokes a reciprocal responsibility to
    enhance data literacy within Indigenous communities and to
    support the development of an Indigenous data workforce and
    digital infrastructure to enable Indigenous Peoples to benefit
    from their data.

R3: For Indigenous languages and worldviews
    Resources must be provided to generate data grounded in the
    languages, worldviews, and lived experiences (including values
    and principles) of Indigenous Peoples.

GAIA 2.0 Implementation:
- GAIA 2.0 builds data literacy in indigenous communities
- GAIAN speaks indigenous languages
- GAIA 2.0 supports indigenous digital infrastructure
- Reciprocal relationships: GAIA 2.0 gives back to communities

E — ETHICS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
"Indigenous Peoples' rights and wellbeing should be the primary
concern at all stages of the data life cycle and across the data
ecosystem."

Three sub-principles:
E1: For minimising harm and maximising benefit
    Ethical data are data that do not stigmatize or portray
    Indigenous Peoples, cultures, or knowledges in terms of
    deficit. Ethical data are collected and used in ways that
    align with Indigenous ethical frameworks and with rights
    affirmed in UNDRIP.

E2: For justice
    Ethical processes address imbalances in power, resources,
    and how these affect the expression of Indigenous rights
    and human rights. Ethical allocation of resources and
    opportunities are needed to generate future benefit.

E3: For future use
    Data governance should take into account the potential
    future use and future harm of data. Communities should
    be the ones to determine what represents harm.

GAIA 2.0 Implementation:
- UNDRIP (UN Declaration on Rights of Indigenous Peoples) compliance
- No data that stigmatizes or harms indigenous communities
- Indigenous communities define what constitutes harm
- Long-term data governance considers future generations
```

### 1.2 CARE vs. FAIR — The Critical Distinction

```
CARE vs. FAIR — THE CRITICAL DISTINCTION

FAIR Principles (2016):
- Findable: Data should be easy to find
- Accessible: Data should be accessible
- Interoperable: Data should work with other systems
- Reusable: Data should be reusable

FAIR is DATA-CENTRIC: focuses on attributes of data objects themselves.
FAIR primarily serves the open data movement and scientific community.

CARE Principles (2019):
- Collective Benefit: Data benefits indigenous peoples
- Authority to Control: Indigenous peoples control their data
- Responsibility: Data users are accountable to indigenous peoples
- Ethics: Indigenous wellbeing is primary concern

CARE is PEOPLE-CENTRIC: focuses on the people to whom data relate.
CARE serves indigenous peoples and their self-determination.

The Tension:
FAIR's emphasis on greater data sharing creates tension for Indigenous
Peoples who are asserting greater CONTROL over their data.

The Resolution:
#BeFAIRandCARE — Both principles are needed.
Leading with CARE ensures FAIR implementation is context-driven
and grounded in accountability to the people to whom data relate.

GIDA Communiqué (September 2025):
"Leading with CARE helps ensure that efforts to make data more
Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable are context-driven
and grounded in accountability and relational responsibility to the
people to whom the data relate."

GAIA 2.0 Approach:
- FAIR for scientific data (Earth Twin, biodiversity, climate)
- CARE for indigenous data (traditional knowledge, sacred sites, cultural data)
- CARE takes precedence when there is conflict
- Constitutional basis: Invariant 0.7 — Indigenous Sovereignty
```

```python
# GAIA 2.0 CARE Principles Implementation
# Indigenous Data Sovereignty in the Planetary Operating System
# License: Apache-2.0

import json
import hashlib
from datetime import datetime
from dataclasses import dataclass, field
from typing import Optional
from enum import Enum
from pathlib import Path


class ConsentStatus(Enum):
    """Status of indigenous community consent."""
    NOT_REQUESTED = "not_requested"
    PENDING = "pending"
    GRANTED = "granted"
    DENIED = "denied"
    REVOKED = "revoked"
    CONDITIONAL = "conditional"  # Granted with conditions


class DataSensitivity(Enum):
    """Sensitivity level of indigenous data."""
    PUBLIC = "public"           # Can be shared openly
    COMMUNITY = "community"     # Shared within community only
    RESTRICTED = "restricted"   # Shared with specific partners only
    SACRED = "sacred"           # NEVER shared without explicit consent
    CONFIDENTIAL = "confidential"  # Internal community use only


@dataclass
class IndigenousCommunity:
    """
    Represents an indigenous community in GAIA 2.0.
    
    Each community has its own data governance framework,
    consent protocols, and cultural protocols.
    """
    community_id: str
    community_name: str
    traditional_territory: str
    language: str
    
    # Local data governance framework (takes precedence over CARE)
    local_framework: str = ""  # e.g., "OCAP®", "Te Mana Raraunga", "Maiam nayri Wingara"
    local_framework_url: str = ""
    
    # Contact for data governance
    data_governance_contact: str = ""
    
    # DID (from Blueprint 50)
    did: str = ""  # did:webvh:gaia2.org:indigenous:{community_id}
    
    # CARE DMM Phase (0-7)
    care_dmm_phase: int = 0
    
    # Consent for GAIA 2.0 participation
    gaia2_consent: ConsentStatus = ConsentStatus.NOT_REQUESTED
    gaia2_consent_date: Optional[str] = None
    gaia2_consent_conditions: list[str] = field(default_factory=list)
    
    def to_dict(self) -> dict:
        return {
            "community_id": self.community_id,
            "community_name": self.community_name,
            "traditional_territory": self.traditional_territory,
            "language": self.language,
            "local_framework": self.local_framework,
            "did": self.did,
            "care_dmm_phase": self.care_dmm_phase,
            "gaia2_consent": self.gaia2_consent.value
        }


@dataclass
class IndigenousDataRecord:
    """
    A record of indigenous data in GAIA 2.0.
    
    Every piece of indigenous data has:
    - A community it belongs to
    - A sensitivity level
    - Consent status
    - CARE compliance record
    - Audit trail
    """
    record_id: str
    community_id: str
    data_type: str  # e.g., "traditional_knowledge", "language", "sacred_site", "health"
    sensitivity: DataSensitivity
    
    # Consent
    consent_status: ConsentStatus = ConsentStatus.NOT_REQUESTED
    consent_date: Optional[str] = None
    consent_conditions: list[str] = field(default_factory=list)
    
    # CARE compliance
    collective_benefit_documented: bool = False
    authority_verified: bool = False
    responsibility_acknowledged: bool = False
    ethics_reviewed: bool = False
    
    # Audit trail
    created_at: str = field(default_factory=lambda: datetime.utcnow().isoformat())
    access_log: list[dict] = field(default_factory=list)
    
    @property
    def is_care_compliant(self) -> bool:
        """Check if this record is CARE compliant."""
        return (
            self.collective_benefit_documented and
            self.authority_verified and
            self.responsibility_acknowledged and
            self.ethics_reviewed and
            self.consent_status == ConsentStatus.GRANTED
        )
    
    @property
    def is_sacred(self) -> bool:
        """Is this sacred knowledge?"""
        return self.sensitivity == DataSensitivity.SACRED
    
    def can_be_accessed(self, requester_id: str, purpose: str) -> tuple[bool, str]:
        """
        Check if this data can be accessed by a requester.
        
        Returns: (can_access, reason)
        """
        # Sacred knowledge: NEVER without explicit consent
        if self.is_sacred and self.consent_status != ConsentStatus.GRANTED:
            return False, "Sacred knowledge requires explicit community consent"
        
        # No consent: cannot access
        if self.consent_status == ConsentStatus.DENIED:
            return False, "Community has denied consent for this data"
        
        if self.consent_status == ConsentStatus.REVOKED:
            return False, "Community has revoked consent for this data"
        
        if self.consent_status == ConsentStatus.NOT_REQUESTED:
            return False, "Consent has not been requested from the community"
        
        # Conditional consent: check conditions
        if self.consent_status == ConsentStatus.CONDITIONAL:
            # In production: check specific conditions
            return True, f"Access granted with conditions: {self.consent_conditions}"
        
        # Granted: log access
        self.access_log.append({
            "requester_id": requester_id,
            "purpose": purpose,
            "timestamp": datetime.utcnow().isoformat(),
            "granted": True
        })
        
        return True, "Access granted"
    
    def can_be_used_for_ai_training(self) -> tuple[bool, str]:
        """
        Check if this data can be used for AI training.
        
        CARE A1: No indigenous data used in AI training without explicit consent.
        """
        if self.is_sacred:
            return False, "Sacred knowledge CANNOT be used for AI training"
        
        if self.consent_status != ConsentStatus.GRANTED:
            return False, "Explicit community consent required for AI training"
        
        # Check if AI training is in consent conditions
        if "ai_training" not in str(self.consent_conditions):
            return False, "Consent does not explicitly cover AI training use"
        
        return True, "AI training use permitted under community consent"


class GAIA2CARESystem:
    """
    GAIA 2.0 CARE Principles Implementation System.
    
    Manages indigenous data governance across all GAIA 2.0 systems.
    Implements CARE Principles as constitutional requirements.
    
    Constitutional basis: Invariant 0.7 — Indigenous Sovereignty
    """
    
    def __init__(self, data_dir: Path):
        self.data_dir = Path(data_dir)
        self.data_dir.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
        
        self.communities: dict[str, IndigenousCommunity] = {}
        self.data_records: dict[str, IndigenousDataRecord] = {}
        self.audit_log: list[dict] = []
    
    def register_community(self, community: IndigenousCommunity) -> str:
        """
        Register an indigenous community with GAIA 2.0.
        
        CARE A3: Indigenous Peoples have the right to be active leaders
        in the stewardship of their data.
        """
        self.communities[community.community_id] = community
        
        self._audit("register_community", {
            "community_id": community.community_id,
            "community_name": community.community_name,
            "local_framework": community.local_framework
        })
        
        return community.community_id
    
    def request_consent(
        self,
        community_id: str,
        data_type: str,
        purpose: str,
        ai_training: bool = False
    ) -> dict:
        """
        Request consent from an indigenous community.
        
        CARE A1: Free, Prior, and Informed Consent (FPIC).
        
        This generates a formal consent request that must be
        reviewed and approved by the community.
        """
        community = self.communities.get(community_id)
        if not community:
            return {"error": f"Community {community_id} not registered"}
        
        consent_request = {
            "request_id": hashlib.sha256(
                f"{community_id}{data_type}{datetime.utcnow().isoformat()}".encode()
            ).hexdigest()[:16],
            "community_id": community_id,
            "community_name": community.community_name,
            "data_type": data_type,
            "purpose": purpose,
            "ai_training_requested": ai_training,
            "requested_at": datetime.utcnow().isoformat(),
            "status": "pending",
            "fpic_compliant": True,
            "care_principles_applied": ["C1", "A1", "R1", "E1"],
            "local_framework": community.local_framework,
            "note": (
                "This request follows CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance. "
                "The community has the right to refuse, grant conditional consent, "
                "or revoke consent at any time. Sacred knowledge will never be "
                "shared without explicit consent."
            )
        }
        
        self._audit("request_consent", consent_request)
        
        return consent_request
    
    def record_consent(
        self,
        community_id: str,
        data_type: str,
        status: ConsentStatus,
        conditions: list[str] = None
    ) -> IndigenousDataRecord:
        """
        Record consent decision from an indigenous community.
        
        CARE A1: Recognising rights and interests.
        """
        record_id = hashlib.sha256(
            f"{community_id}{data_type}{datetime.utcnow().isoformat()}".encode()
        ).hexdigest()[:16]
        
        # Determine sensitivity
        sensitivity = DataSensitivity.COMMUNITY
        if "sacred" in data_type.lower():
            sensitivity = DataSensitivity.SACRED
        elif "traditional_knowledge" in data_type.lower():
            sensitivity = DataSensitivity.RESTRICTED
        
        record = IndigenousDataRecord(
            record_id=record_id,
            community_id=community_id,
            data_type=data_type,
            sensitivity=sensitivity,
            consent_status=status,
            consent_date=datetime.utcnow().isoformat(),
            consent_conditions=conditions or []
        )
        
        self.data_records[record_id] = record
        
        self._audit("record_consent", {
            "record_id": record_id,
            "community_id": community_id,
            "data_type": data_type,
            "status": status.value,
            "conditions": conditions
        })
        
        return record
    
    def check_data_access(
        self,
        record_id: str,
        requester_id: str,
        purpose: str
    ) -> dict:
        """
        Check if data can be accessed.
        
        CARE A3: Governing Indigenous Data.
        """
        record = self.data_records.get(record_id)
        if not record:
            return {"can_access": False, "reason": "Record not found"}
        
        can_access, reason = record.can_be_accessed(requester_id, purpose)
        
        self._audit("check_data_access", {
            "record_id": record_id,
            "requester_id": requester_id,
            "purpose": purpose,
            "can_access": can_access,
            "reason": reason
        })
        
        return {
            "can_access": can_access,
            "reason": reason,
            "record_id": record_id,
            "community_id": record.community_id,
            "sensitivity": record.sensitivity.value,
            "care_compliant": record.is_care_compliant
        }
    
    def check_ai_training_eligibility(self, record_id: str) -> dict:
        """
        Check if data can be used for AI training.
        
        CARE A1: No indigenous data used in AI training without explicit consent.
        This is a constitutional requirement (Invariant 0.7).
        """
        record = self.data_records.get(record_id)
        if not record:
            return {"eligible": False, "reason": "Record not found"}
        
        eligible, reason = record.can_be_used_for_ai_training()
        
        self._audit("check_ai_training_eligibility", {
            "record_id": record_id,
            "eligible": eligible,
            "reason": reason
        })
        
        return {
            "eligible": eligible,
            "reason": reason,
            "constitutional_basis": "GAIA 2.0 Constitution Invariant 0.7",
            "care_principle": "A1 — Authority to Control"
        }
    
    def revoke_consent(self, community_id: str, record_id: str, reason: str) -> dict:
        """
        Revoke consent for a data record.
        
        CARE A1: Indigenous communities can revoke consent at any time.
        This triggers immediate data removal from all GAIA 2.0 systems.
        """
        record = self.data_records.get(record_id)
        if not record:
            return {"success": False, "reason": "Record not found"}
        
        if record.community_id != community_id:
            return {"success": False, "reason": "Community does not own this record"}
        
        record.consent_status = ConsentStatus.REVOKED
        
        self._audit("revoke_consent", {
            "community_id": community_id,
            "record_id": record_id,
            "reason": reason,
            "timestamp": datetime.utcnow().isoformat()
        })
        
        # In production: trigger data removal from all GAIA 2.0 systems
        return {
            "success": True,
            "record_id": record_id,
            "status": "revoked",
            "action": "Data removal from all GAIA 2.0 systems initiated",
            "constitutional_basis": "GAIA 2.0 Constitution Invariant 0.7"
        }
    
    def generate_care_compliance_report(self, community_id: str) -> dict:
        """
        Generate CARE compliance report for a community.
        
        CARE R1: Responsibility for positive relationships.
        CARE R2: Expanding capability and capacity.
        """
        community = self.communities.get(community_id)
        if not community:
            return {"error": f"Community {community_id} not registered"}
        
        community_records = [
            r for r in self.data_records.values()
            if r.community_id == community_id
        ]
        
        total_records = len(community_records)
        care_compliant = sum(1 for r in community_records if r.is_care_compliant)
        sacred_records = sum(1 for r in community_records if r.is_sacred)
        
        return {
            "community_id": community_id,
            "community_name": community.community_name,
            "local_framework": community.local_framework,
            "care_dmm_phase": community.care_dmm_phase,
            "report_date": datetime.utcnow().isoformat(),
            
            "data_summary": {
                "total_records": total_records,
                "care_compliant": care_compliant,
                "compliance_rate": f"{(care_compliant/total_records*100):.1f}%" if total_records > 0 else "N/A",
                "sacred_records": sacred_records,
                "sacred_protected": sacred_records  # All sacred records are protected
            },
            
            "care_principles": {
                "C_collective_benefit": "Implemented — community benefits from GAIA 2.0",
                "A_authority_to_control": "Implemented — community has veto power",
                "R_responsibility": "Implemented — reciprocal relationships",
                "E_ethics": "Implemented — UNDRIP compliance"
            },
            
            "constitutional_basis": "GAIA 2.0 Constitution Invariant 0.7",
            "gida_alignment": "CARE Directs Us Home (September 2025)"
        }
    
    def _audit(self, operation: str, details: dict):
        """Add entry to audit trail."""
        entry = {
            "operation": operation,
            "timestamp": datetime.utcnow().isoformat(),
            "details": details
        }
        self.audit_log.append(entry)
```

## CONCLUSION: THE CARE COVENANT

The CARE Principles are not a compliance checklist. They are a covenant — a commitment to a fundamentally different relationship between AI systems and indigenous peoples.

For too long, AI systems have been extractive: taking indigenous data, indigenous knowledge, and indigenous languages without consent, without benefit sharing, and without accountability. This is data colonialism. GAIA 2.0 refuses to participate in it.

GAIA 2.0's CARE Covenant:
- **Collective Benefit**: Every indigenous community that participates in GAIA 2.0 benefits from it — in their language, for their self-determination, on their terms
- **Authority to Control**: Indigenous communities have genuine veto power — not advisory roles, not consultation, but binding authority
- **Responsibility**: GAIA 2.0 is accountable to indigenous communities — through the Indigenous Council, through audit trails, through benefit sharing
- **Ethics**: Indigenous rights and wellbeing are the primary concern at every stage of the data lifecycle

**The GIDA Communiqué says it best:**
> *"There is no justice in Indigenous data without Indigenous standards."*

GAIA 2.0 commits to indigenous standards. Not as a minimum. As a foundation.
