### 3.1 GAIA 2.0 NSF PESOSE Track 2 Application

```
NSF PESOSE TRACK 2 APPLICATION FRAMEWORK
"GAIA 2.0 Earth Twin: Secure Open-Source Ecosystem for Planetary Health"

PROJECT TITLE:
GAIA 2.0 Earth Twin: A Secure, Sustainable Open-Source Ecosystem
for Real-Time Planetary Health Intelligence

ABSTRACT (300 words):
The GAIA 2.0 Earth Twin is an open-source planetary health monitoring
system that integrates data from Destination Earth (DestinE), Copernicus,
GBIF, USGS, and NOAA into a unified, AI-powered Earth intelligence platform.
It provides real-time monitoring of climate tipping points, biodiversity,
extreme weather, and ocean health — freely accessible to every scientist,
policymaker, and citizen on Earth.

This project establishes GAIA 2.0 Earth Twin as a secure, sustainable
open-source ecosystem (OSE) under the Apache Software Foundation. We will:
(1) Implement security infrastructure (Asterinas memory-safe kernel;
    Apache Trusted Releases; SBOM; SLSA Level 2 provenance)
(2) Build sustainable community governance (Apache incubation;
    Indigenous Council; democratic governance)
(3) Scale to planetary deployment (10 country nodes; 100K users)
(4) Ensure equitable access (free; multilingual; indigenous language support)

The GAIA 2.0 Earth Twin addresses an emergent societal need: humanity
needs real-time, open, trustworthy planetary health intelligence to
respond to climate change, biodiversity loss, and tipping points.
Current solutions are either proprietary, expensive, or inaccessible
to the Global South. GAIA 2.0 changes this.

INTELLECTUAL MERIT:
- Novel integration of heterogeneous Earth data (ESFM; arXiv:2605.00850)
- Ultra-early tipping point prediction (arXiv:2603.14944)
- Animal communication AI (NatureLM-audio; ICLR 2025)
- Memory-safe OS kernel (Asterinas; USENIX ATC 2025)

BROADER IMPACTS:
- Free planetary health intelligence for all humanity
- Indigenous data sovereignty (CARE principles)
- Democratic AI governance (Democracy Level 4 by 2028)
- Net-zero carbon by 2030

BUDGET: $1,500,000 over 36 months
TRACK: Track 2 (Establish and expand sustainable OSE)
DEADLINE: March 2, 2027
```
