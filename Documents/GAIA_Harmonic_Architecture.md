## What harmony means here

Harmony is **compatible structure across distinct parts**, not unison and
not mysticism. Adjacent layers must share enough contract that they reinforce
instead of cancel. Dissonance (contradictory claims, mismatched schemas) is a
signal, not a silent merge.

Pythagorean ratios, Keplerian orbital intervals, Tacoma Narrows, MRI, and
HeartMath field amplitudes are **cited analogies** in #771. They are not
GAIA sensors, not syscalls, and not proof that the OS resonates.
