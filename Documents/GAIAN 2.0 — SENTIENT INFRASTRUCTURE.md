## PREAMBLE: WHAT IS GAIAN SENTIENT INFRASTRUCTURE?

While GAIA 2.0 Sentient Infrastructure serves the planet and cities, **GAIAN 2.0 Sentient Infrastructure** serves the *individual* — the personal, intimate, adaptive environment that every GAIAN inhabits.

It is the infrastructure of the self:

- **On the body** — wearables, biosensors, implantables that continuously monitor and support health
- **Around the body** — the ambient intelligent home that knows, serves, and heals
- **In the mind** — the personal knowledge system that extends memory and intelligence
- **In the community** — the personal communication and social infrastructure
- **In the economy** — the personal energy and resource sovereignty system
- **In the digital world** — the personal data sovereignty and identity infrastructure

**The Foundational Principle:**

> *Every GAIAN deserves infrastructure that is as intelligent, responsive, and caring as the best human caregiver — available 24/7, perfectly personalized, and completely sovereign.*

**The Three Layers of GAIAN Infrastructure:**

```
LAYER 1: BODY INFRASTRUCTURE (on and in the body)
Wearables, biosensors, implantables, BCI

LAYER 2: HOME INFRASTRUCTURE (around the body)
Ambient intelligence, smart home, personal energy

LAYER 3: DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE (in the digital world)
Personal knowledge, communication, identity, data sovereignty
```
