## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The ocean covers 71% of Earth's surface. It contains 97% of Earth's water. It produces 50% of Earth's oxygen. It absorbs 25% of all CO₂ emissions and 90% of excess heat from climate change. It is home to 80%+ of all life on Earth — most of it still undiscovered. It regulates global climate, drives weather patterns, feeds 3+ billion people, and supports the livelihoods of hundreds of millions more.

Yet the ocean is the least monitored, least understood, and most threatened system on Earth. Less than 25% of the ocean floor has been mapped in detail. Less than 10% of the deep ocean has been explored. The ocean is warming, acidifying, losing oxygen, and losing biodiversity at rates unprecedented in human history.

GAIA 2.0 treats the ocean not as a separate domain but as the **planetary circulatory system** — the blood of the living Earth. Without a healthy ocean, there is no healthy planet.

**The Ocean in Numbers (2026):**
- 361 million km² of ocean surface; average depth 3,688 meters
