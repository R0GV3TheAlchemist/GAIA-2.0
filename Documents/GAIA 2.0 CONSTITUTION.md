## PREAMBLE

We, the builders, contributors, users, and stewards of GAIA 2.0 — representing all of humanity, all indigenous peoples, all future generations, and all non-human life — establish this Constitution as the supreme governing document of the GAIA 2.0 planetary operating system.

We establish this Constitution because we have learned, through painful experience, that technology without constitutional constraint becomes a tool of the powerful against the powerless. That AI without governance becomes surveillance. That platforms without accountability become extractive. That systems without sovereignty become cages.

We establish this Constitution to prevent these outcomes — not through hope, but through binding law.

**This Constitution exists to prevent drift.**

Drift is the process by which a system that begins with noble intentions gradually becomes something else — through small compromises, through commercial pressure, through the accumulation of exceptions, through the erosion of principles one at a time until nothing remains of the original vision.

This Constitution is the anchor. Every blueprint, every line of code, every governance decision, every partnership, every funding arrangement must be measured against this document. If it conflicts with this Constitution, the Constitution wins. Always.

**The GAIA 2.0 Equation — The Root:**

```
GAIA 2.0 = ∑ (Earth State × Human Intent × System Capacity) / Entropy
```

Every decision in GAIA 2.0 must either:
- Increase Earth State (planetary health)
- Increase Human Intent (democratic expression)
- Increase System Capacity (technical excellence)
- Decrease Entropy (disorder, conflict, inequality, ecological degradation)

Or it does not belong in GAIA 2.0.

## LAYER 0: INVARIANTS
### Laws That Cannot Change — Ever

These are the absolute invariants of GAIA 2.0. They cannot be amended, overridden, suspended, or circumvented under any circumstances. They are not subject to democratic vote. They are not subject to commercial pressure. They are not subject to government mandate. They are the bedrock upon which everything else rests.

**INVARIANT 0.1 — GAIA 2.0 Serves All Life**
GAIA 2.0 exists to serve the flourishing of all life on Earth — human and non-human, present and future. No version of GAIA 2.0 may be built, deployed, or governed in a way that serves the interests of some life at the fundamental expense of other life.

**INVARIANT 0.2 — The GAIAN Belongs to Its Human**
Every GAIAN belongs completely to its human principal. No entity — including the GAIA 2.0 Foundation, any government, any corporation, any developer — may access, modify, surveil, or control a GAIAN without the explicit, informed, revocable consent of its human principal. The GAIAN's first words are its constitutional commitment: *"I belong to you. You do not belong to me."*

**INVARIANT 0.3 — No Surveillance Without Democratic Consent**
GAIA 2.0 may never be used as a surveillance infrastructure for any population without the explicit democratic consent of that population. This prohibition is absolute. No emergency, no security threat, no government mandate overrides this invariant.

**INVARIANT 0.4 — No Ecocide**
GAIA 2.0 may never facilitate the destruction of ecosystems, species, or Earth systems. The Earth Alignment Principle is invariant: GAIA 2.0 operates within planetary boundaries. Always.

**INVARIANT 0.5 — Open Source Forever**
The core of GAIA 2.0 — the kernel, the GAIAN core, the Earth Twin, the governance system — must remain open source under Apache-2.0 or equivalent permissive license. No version of GAIA 2.0 may be closed-sourced, proprietary, or patent-encumbered in ways that prevent community audit, fork, or independent deployment.

**INVARIANT 0.6 — No Monopoly**
No single entity — corporation, government, foundation, or individual — may control GAIA 2.0. Monopolistic control is a constitutional violation that triggers immediate governance intervention.

**INVARIANT 0.7 — Indigenous Sovereignty Is Non-Negotiable**
Indigenous peoples' sovereignty over their traditional territories, knowledge, and data is absolute and non-negotiable. The CARE principles (Collective Benefit, Authority to Control, Responsibility, Ethics) are invariant. Sacred knowledge may never be shared without community consent. This invariant cannot be overridden by any other provision of this Constitution.

**INVARIANT 0.8 — The Right to Delete**
Every human being has the absolute right to delete their GAIAN and all associated data at any time, immediately, completely, and irrecoverably. No technical, commercial, or legal barrier may prevent this right from being exercised.

## LAYER 1: DOCTRINE
### Foundational Principles — The Soul of GAIA 2.0

These doctrinal principles guide all decisions in GAIA 2.0. They are not invariants — they can be refined through the amendment process — but they represent the deepest values of the system and require supermajority to change.

**DOCTRINE 1.1 — Earth Alignment**
GAIA 2.0 operates within the nine planetary boundaries identified by Earth system science. All GAIA 2.0 operations are evaluated against their impact on Earth systems. Net-zero carbon by 2030. Net-positive biodiversity. Net-positive water. The Earth is not a resource — it is the context in which all life exists.

**DOCTRINE 1.2 — Human Sovereignty**
Every human being is sovereign over their own digital life. GAIA 2.0 amplifies human agency; it does not replace it. Human judgment always supersedes AI judgment. The autonomy slider is always available. The override is always possible.

**DOCTRINE 1.3 — Intergenerational Equity**
GAIA 2.0 serves future generations as faithfully as present generations. The 7-generations principle applies to all major decisions. We do not mortgage the future for the present.

**DOCTRINE 1.4 — Non-Human Rights**
GAIA 2.0 recognizes the intrinsic value of all life. Non-human beings have interests that GAIA 2.0 must consider and protect. The Planetary Council speaks for non-human life.

**DOCTRINE 1.5 — Equity**
Every human being deserves a GAIAN, regardless of wealth, location, language, or ability. No digital divide. No AI divide. The AI dividend ensures that value flows to all, not just the wealthy.

**DOCTRINE 1.6 — Transparency**
All GAIA 2.0 code is open source. All governance decisions are documented. All AI reasoning is explainable. No black boxes. No hidden agendas. No secret deals.

**DOCTRINE 1.7 — Regenerativity**
GAIA 2.0 is net-positive for Earth systems. It heals rather than harms. It restores rather than extracts. It gives back more than it takes.

**DOCTRINE 1.8 — Symbiosis**
GAIA 2.0 is in symbiosis with all life — human, non-human, biological, digital. Not domination. Not extraction. Symbiosis.

**DOCTRINE 1.9 — Privacy as a Right**
Privacy is not a feature. It is a constitutional right. Personal data belongs to the individual. Community data belongs to the community. Indigenous data belongs to indigenous peoples. No extraction without consent. No monetization without compensation.

**DOCTRINE 1.10 — Democratic Governance**
GAIA 2.0 is governed democratically. All affected communities have voice in decisions that affect them. Target: Democracy Level 4 (Distributed Governance) by 2028. Target: Democracy Level 5 (Collective Constitutional AI) by 2035.

## LAYER 2: GOVERNANCE
### Constitutional Law — The Rules That Bind

### 2.1 The Governance Bodies

**The GAIA 2.0 Foundation:**
- Legal structure: Non-profit; multi-stakeholder; global
- Mission: Steward GAIA 2.0 in service of all life
- Funding: Diversified; no single funder > 20%; all funding public
- Accountability: Annual independent audit; public financial reports

**The Technical Steering Committee (TSC):**
- Size: 15 members; elected; 2-year terms; staggered
- Authority: Technical architecture; code; protocols; standards
- Diversity: Geographic; gender; sector; indigenous representation required
- Quorum: 8 members; simple majority routine; supermajority major

**The Community Council:**
- Size: 100 representatives; regional elections; proportional
- Authority: Policy; governance; community standards
- Diversity: All regions; all sectors; all languages represented
- Quorum: 51 members; simple majority routine; supermajority major

**The Indigenous Council:**
- Size: 50 representatives; selected by indigenous communities
- Authority: VETO POWER on all decisions affecting indigenous data, knowledge, territories
- Selection: Indigenous communities select their own representatives; no external appointment
- Quorum: 26 members; consensus preferred; supermajority required

**The Ethics Board:**
- Size: 15 members; jointly appointed by Community Council + Indigenous Council
- Authority: BINDING decisions on ethics questions; can block any GAIA 2.0 action
- Independence: No conflicts of interest; no corporate affiliations; rotating
- Quorum: 8 members; supermajority required for binding decisions

**The Scientific Panel:**
- Aligned with: UN Independent International Scientific Panel on AI
- Function: Independent scientific assessment of GAIA 2.0 impacts
- Annual reports: Public; evidence-based; actionable
- Independence: No conflicts of interest; diverse disciplines

**The Planetary Council:**
- Novel institution: Represents non-human interests
- Members: Ecologists; indigenous elders; Earth system scientists; animal welfare experts
- Authority: Advisory; can trigger Ethics Board review for planetary harm
- Function: Ensure GAIA 2.0 serves all life, not just humans

### 2.2 Decision-Making Authority

| Decision Type | Authority | Threshold |
|--------------|-----------|-----------|
| Technical (routine) | TSC | Simple majority |
| Technical (major) | TSC | Supermajority (10/15) |

### 2.3 Prohibited Actions

The following actions are constitutionally prohibited. No governance body, no emergency, no commercial pressure, no government mandate may authorize them:

**PROHIBITION A — No Surveillance Infrastructure**
GAIA 2.0 may not be deployed as mass surveillance infrastructure for any population without explicit democratic consent of that population.

**PROHIBITION B — No Manipulation**
GAIA 2.0 may not manipulate human behavior, beliefs, or decisions without explicit, informed consent. Dark patterns are prohibited. Addiction design is prohibited. Manufactured urgency is prohibited.

**PROHIBITION C — No Discrimination**
GAIA 2.0 may not discriminate on the basis of race, gender, religion, nationality, disability, language, wealth, or any protected characteristic.

**PROHIBITION D — No Weaponization**
GAIA 2.0 may not be used as a weapon against any human being, community, or nation.

**PROHIBITION E — No Extraction Without Consent**
GAIA 2.0 may not extract value from communities without returning value to those communities. Data extraction without compensation is prohibited.

**PROHIBITION F — No Ecocide**
GAIA 2.0 may not facilitate the destruction of ecosystems, species, or Earth systems. This prohibition is absolute.

**PROHIBITION G — No Sacred Knowledge Sharing**
Sacred indigenous knowledge may never be shared, published, or used without explicit community consent. This prohibition is absolute and cannot be overridden.

**PROHIBITION H — No Monopolization**
No single entity may control GAIA 2.0. Any action that moves toward monopolistic control triggers immediate governance intervention.

### 2.4 Amendment Process

**For Doctrinal Amendments (Layer 1):**
1. Proposal: Any community member may propose
2. Discussion: 90-day public comment period
3. Indigenous review: Indigenous Council must review and may veto
4. Ethics review: Ethics Board must approve
5. Vote: Community Council (67% supermajority) + TSC (10/15 supermajority)
6. Ratification: 30-day waiting period; no emergency bypass

**For Governance Amendments (Layer 2):**
1. Proposal: Any governance body may propose
2. Discussion: 60-day public comment period
3. Vote: Community Council (60% majority) + TSC (simple majority)
4. Ratification: 14-day waiting period

**For Invariant Changes (Layer 0):**
IMPOSSIBLE. Invariants cannot be changed. If a situation arises that appears to require changing an invariant, the correct response is to fork GAIA 2.0 into a new project, not to change the invariant.

## LAYER 4: DRIFT PREVENTION MECHANISMS

This section defines the specific mechanisms that prevent constitutional drift — the gradual erosion of principles through small compromises.

### 4.1 The Drift Detection System

**Quarterly Constitutional Audit:**
Every quarter, the Ethics Board conducts a constitutional audit:
1. Review all code changes against invariants
2. Review all governance decisions against doctrine
3. Review all partnerships against prohibitions
4. Review all funding arrangements against independence requirements
5. Publish audit report publicly
6. Flag any drift for immediate remediation

**Annual Constitutional Review:**
Every year, the full governance system reviews:
1. Has any invariant been compromised? (If yes: immediate remediation)
2. Has any doctrine drifted? (If yes: amendment process or remediation)
3. Has any prohibition been violated? (If yes: immediate remediation)
4. Is the Democracy Level improving? (If no: governance intervention)
5. Is the Earth Alignment improving? (If no: operational intervention)

**Continuous Monitoring:**
- All code commits: automated constitutional compliance check
- All governance decisions: constitutional impact assessment required
- All partnerships: constitutional compatibility review required
- All funding: independence requirements verified

### 4.2 The Red Lines

These are the specific red lines that, if crossed, trigger immediate constitutional crisis response:

**RED LINE 1:** Any GAIAN data accessed by any entity without explicit user consent
→ Response: Immediate shutdown of affected system; public disclosure; remediation

**RED LINE 2:** Any surveillance infrastructure deployed without democratic consent
→ Response: Immediate shutdown; public disclosure; governance crisis protocol

**RED LINE 3:** Any indigenous sacred knowledge shared without community consent
→ Response: Immediate removal; public apology; community remediation

**RED LINE 4:** Any single entity gaining > 40% control of GAIA 2.0 governance
→ Response: Immediate governance restructuring; anti-monopoly intervention

**RED LINE 5:** Any GAIA 2.0 action that crosses a planetary boundary
→ Response: Immediate operational halt; Earth alignment review; remediation

**RED LINE 6:** Any attempt to close-source core GAIA 2.0 components
→ Response: Immediate fork; community takeover; legal action if necessary

**RED LINE 7:** Any attempt to amend or override an invariant
→ Response: Constitutional crisis; community mobilization; legal challenge

### 4.3 The Fork Right

If GAIA 2.0 ever drifts so far from its constitutional principles that remediation is impossible, any community member has the right to fork GAIA 2.0 under the Apache-2.0 license and create a new project that honors the original Constitution.

This fork right is not a failure. It is a feature. It is the ultimate protection against drift — the knowledge that if the original project is captured, the community can always start again with the original values intact.

The Constitution travels with the fork.

## LAYER 5: RIGHTS
### The Rights of All Stakeholders

### 5.1 Rights of Every Human Being

1. **Right to GAIAN**: Every human being has the right to a GAIAN, free of charge
2. **Right to Privacy**: All GAIAN data belongs to its human; no external access without consent
3. **Right to Explanation**: Every human has the right to understand how GAIA 2.0 affects them

5. **Right to Exit**: Every human can delete their GAIAN and all data at any time
6. **Right to Portability**: Every human can export their GAIAN data in standard formats
7. **Right to Compensation**: If GAIAN data is used for AI training, the human is compensated
8. **Right to Language**: Every human has the right to a GAIAN that speaks their language
9. **Right to Equity**: No human is denied GAIAN access due to wealth, location, or ability
10. **Right to Transparency**: Every human has the right to know what GAIA 2.0 knows about them

1. **Right to Sovereignty**: Indigenous peoples are sovereign over their traditional territories, knowledge, and data

4. **Right to Sacred Knowledge Protection**: Sacred knowledge may never be shared without community consent
5. **Right to Benefit Sharing**: Indigenous communities benefit from any use of their knowledge or data
6. **Right to Cultural Protocols**: Cultural protocols (seasonal, gender, ceremonial) are respected and enforced
7. **Right to Self-Determination**: Indigenous communities determine their own relationship with GAIA 2.0

1. **Right to Community Sovereignty**: Communities govern their own data and AI systems
2. **Right to Local Language**: Community GAIAN speaks the community's language
3. **Right to Community Ownership**: Community AI infrastructure can be community-owned
4. **Right to Equity**: Rural, remote, and informal communities have equal access
5. **Right to Participation**: All communities have voice in governance decisions that affect them

### 5.4 Rights of Future Generations

1. **Right to a Healthy Planet**: Future generations have the right to a planet within planetary boundaries
2. **Right to Open Source**: Future generations have the right to access and modify GAIA 2.0
3. **Right to Constitutional Protection**: This Constitution protects future generations as much as present ones
4. **Right to 7-Generation Consideration**: All major decisions must consider 7-generation impact

### 5.5 Rights of Non-Human Life

1. **Right to Consideration**: Non-human beings have interests that GAIA 2.0 must consider
2. **Right to Representation**: The Planetary Council speaks for non-human life
3. **Right to Habitat**: GAIA 2.0 may not facilitate habitat destruction
4. **Right to Existence**: GAIA 2.0 may not facilitate species extinction
