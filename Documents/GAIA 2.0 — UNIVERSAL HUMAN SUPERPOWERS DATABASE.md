## PREAMBLE: WHAT IS A SUPERPOWER?

In GAIA 2.0, a **Superpower** is any human capability that significantly exceeds the ordinary range of human performance — whether through:

- **Natural variation** (rare genetic gifts, extraordinary neurological wiring)
- **Extreme training** (decades of deliberate practice pushing biological limits)
- **Altered states** (flow, meditation, psychedelics, peak consciousness)
- **Technological augmentation** (BCI, exoskeletons, AI amplification)
- **Collective emergence** (group intelligence exceeding individual capability)
- **Scientific intervention** (longevity, regeneration, genetic enhancement)

**The Three Categories of Human Superpowers:**

```
CATEGORY 1: NATURAL SUPERPOWERS
Already present in some humans — rare genetic gifts, extraordinary
neurological configurations, or extreme biological adaptations.
These exist NOW in the human population.

CATEGORY 2: TRAINED SUPERPOWERS
Capabilities that any human can develop through extreme, dedicated
practice — pushing the boundaries of what the human body and mind
can do. These are ACHIEVABLE by most humans.

CATEGORY 3: AUGMENTED SUPERPOWERS
Capabilities that emerge from the fusion of human biology with
technology — BCI, exoskeletons, AI amplification, genetic
intervention, pharmacological enhancement. These are EMERGING NOW.
```

**The Superpower Spectrum:**
```
ORDINARY → EXCEPTIONAL → ELITE → EXTRAORDINARY → SUPERHUMAN → POSTHUMAN
```
